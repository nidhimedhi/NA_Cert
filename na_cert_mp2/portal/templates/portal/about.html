About NA Certificate

Everything you need to know about Non-Agricultural land conversion in Maharashtra.

📋

Why Do We Need an NA Certificate?

Legalization, financing, and utility benefits.

A Non Agricultural (NA) certificate is the legal backbone for land development. It is required because:

  • ✦
    Legalization: It officially converts land from farming to residential use, preventing government penalties.
  • ✦
    Financing: Banks only approve construction loans and mortgages for NA certified plots.
  • ✦
    Utilities: It is mandatory for obtaining permanent water, drainage, and electricity connections.
  • ✦
    Market Value: Certified land carries a higher resale value and a "clear title" for buyers.
⚙️

The Generation Process

Steps involve application, verification, and NOC clearance.

The conversion process is handled by the Revenue Department through these standard steps:

  • ✦
    Application: Submit a formal request to the District Collector or SDO with land records.
  • ✦
    Verification: The Tahasildar conducts a site visit to verify boundaries and land titles.
  • ✦
    NOC Clearance: Approvals are gathered from Town Planning and the Healthcare Departments.
  • ✦
    Premium Payment: Pay the conversion tax (NA Premium) based on the land's size and location.
  • ✦
    Final Order: The Collector issues the formal NA Certificate and updates the land records.
📏

Rules

Rules for Applying NA Certificate.

  • ✦
    Zoning Compliance: The land must fall within a "convertible zone" as defined by the regional Development Plan (DP).
  • ✦
    Clear Marketable Title: The applicant must be the sole legal owner, and the land must be free from any legal disputes.
  • ✦
    Access & Connectivity: The plot must have a sanctioned approach road of a minimum width to allow for emergency vehicle access.
  • ✦
    No-Objection Certificates (NOCs): Applicants must obtain clearances from the Fire, Pollution Control Board, and Water Resources Department.
  • ✦
    Time-Bound Construction: Once NA status is granted, the owner must begin construction within 1–3 years.
🗂️

Essential Documents

Non compliance leads to legal and financial issues.

  • ✦
    7/12 Extract: An updated government record proving current ownership, survey number, and total land area.
  • ✦
    Mutation Entry (Ferfar): Historical records (Form VI) that document the chain of ownership changes over time.
  • ✦
    Village Form VI: Details regarding the land's history, cultivation status, and any existing liabilities.
  • ✦
    Certified Site Plan: A professional blueprint showing the exact boundaries, surrounding roads, and any existing structures on the plot.
  • ✦
    Zone Certificate: Official proof from the Town Planning authority confirming the land is in a developable zone.
Land Categories

Types of Non-Agricultural Land

Understanding each land category helps you choose the right NA Certificate application process.

🏡
Residential

Residential Non-Agricultural Land

Used for constructing houses, apartments, plotted developments, and housing societies. Once converted, the land can be legally used for building permanent residential structures.

🏠 Individual Single owner applying for personal residential development. Requires ownership document, 7/12 Utara, holding certificate, and proposed layout plan.
🤝 Partnership Two or more co-owners applying jointly. Requires all individual documents plus partnership agreement deed and tax returns.
🎓
Educational

Educational Non-Agricultural Land

Designated for building schools, colleges, universities, coaching centres, and other educational institutions. The process involves Town Planning and the Collector's office.

🏛️ Granted Land directly granted by the government for educational purposes with full state backing and funding support.
🔀 Semi-Granted Partially government-aided educational land with some private ownership involvement and mixed funding.
🏢 Private Entirely privately owned land being converted for educational institution setup without government aid.
🏭
Industrial

Industrial Non-Agricultural Land

Used for factories, manufacturing plants, warehouses, processing units, and industrial estates. Conversion requires strict compliance with environmental and zoning norms.

🏛️ Granted Government-allocated land in designated industrial zones with full state authorization and infrastructure support.
🔀 Semi-Granted Partially state-supported industrial land in developing economic zones with shared ownership.
🏢 Private Privately owned land being legally converted for industrial manufacturing, warehousing, or processing use.
🏢
Commercial

Commercial Non-Agricultural Land

Used for shops, offices, malls, hotels, restaurants, and commercial complexes. This is the most common type of conversion in urban and semi-urban areas of Maharashtra.

🏛️ Granted Government-granted commercial plots in planned development zones with full legal status and utilities.
🔀 Semi-Granted Partially state-allocated commercial land with mixed private and government ownership structure.
🏢 Private Privately owned land converted for commercial business establishment, retail trade, or office use.

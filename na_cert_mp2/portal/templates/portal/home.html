Trend of Non-Agricultural Land

The provided graph illustrates a consistent and significant increase in non-agricultural land area in Maharashtra over the span of 52 years, rising from 864,000 hectares in 1971 to 1,768,000 hectares in 2023.

This steady upward trajectory reflects the state's aggressive shift from a purely agrarian economy toward a modernized landscape dominated by industrialization and urban expansion.

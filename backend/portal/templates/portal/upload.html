Important Instructions

Please read all instructions carefully before proceeding

Applying for: —
📁 Document Requirements
  • 1. All documents must be clear, legible, and complete. Blurred or incomplete documents will be rejected.
  • 2. Accepted file formats are PDF, JPG, and PNG only. Maximum file size is 5MB per document.
  • 3. Each document must be uploaded in its designated section. Do not upload multiple documents in one section.
  • 4. All documents must be self-attested. Government-issued documents must be valid and not expired.
  • 5. The 7/12 Utara (Satbara) must be the latest copy downloaded from the Maharashtra government portal.
✍️ Digital Signature
  • 6. You will be required to provide a digital signature on the application upload page.
  • 7. Your digital signature confirms that all documents submitted are genuine and accurate.
  • 8. Providing a false digital signature is a punishable offence under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
⚙️ Application Process
  • 9. Once submitted, your application will be reviewed by the Tahsildar within 15–30 working days.
  • 10. You will receive a unique reference number after submission. Keep it safe for tracking.
  • 11. If your application is rejected, the reason will be communicated and you may reapply.
  • 12. Do not submit duplicate applications. Multiple submissions for the same land will be rejected.
⚠️ Legal Warning: Submission of forged, altered, or false documents is a criminal offence punishable under IPC Section 420 and the Maharashtra Land Revenue Code. All submitted documents are subject to verification by government authorities.

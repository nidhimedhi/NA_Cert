About Non-Agricultural Land Governance

Non-agricultural land refers to any land area that has been designated or legally converted for purposes other than farming. Using land for these purposes usually requires an official NA certificate from local authorities. Start your journey with us today and secure your piece of the earth.

Our vision is to facilitate sustainable urban development and economic growth by efficiently transforming land use while maintaining ecological balance. We aim to provide a legal, transparent, and structured process for converting agricultural land into productive residential, commercial, or industrial assets.

Why Do We Need an NA Certificate?

A Non Agricultural (NA) certificate is the legal backbone for land development. It is crucial for legalization, financing, and utility benefits. The primary reasons include:

  • Legalization: It officially converts land from farming to residential/commercial use, preventing government penalties.
  • Financing: Banks only approve construction loans and mortgages for NA certified plots.
  • Utilities: It is mandatory for obtaining permanent water, drainage, and electricity connections.
  • Market Value: Certified land carries a higher resale value and a "clear title" for buyers.

The Generation Process

The conversion process is handled by the Revenue Department through these standard legal steps:

  • Application: Submit a formal request to the District Collector or SDO with land records.
  • Verification: The Tahasildar conducts a site visit to verify boundaries and land titles.
  • NOC Clearance: Approvals are gathered from Town Planning and the Healthcare Departments.
  • Premium Payment: Pay the conversion tax (NA Premium) based on the land's size and location.
  • Final Order: The Collector issues the formal NA Certificate and updates the land records.

Rules & Guidelines

Strict rules are implemented to ensure safe and planned development across Maharashtra:

  • Zoning Compliance: The land must fall within a "convertible zone" as defined by the regional Development Plan (DP).
  • Clear Marketable Title: The applicant must be the sole legal owner, free from any disputes.
  • Access & Connectivity: The plot must have a sanctioned approach road of a minimum width.
  • No-Objection Certificates (NOCs): Applicants must obtain clearances from Fire, Pollution Control, and Water Resources.
  • Time-Bound Construction: Once granted, construction must begin within 1–3 years.

Essential Documents

Non compliance with documentation leads to legal and financial issues. You must prepare:

  • 7/12 Extract: Updated government record proving current ownership, survey number, and area.
  • Mutation Entry (Ferfar): Historical records (Form VI) documenting chain of ownership.
  • Village Form VI: Details regarding the land's history, cultivation status, and liabilities.
  • Certified Site Plan: A professional blueprint showing boundaries and surrounding roads.
  • Zone Certificate: Official proof from Town Planning confirming a developable zone.

Types of Non-Agricultural Land

Understanding each land category helps you choose the right NA Certificate application process.

Residential Non-Agricultural Land

Used for constructing houses, apartments, plotted developments, and housing societies. Once converted, the land can be legally used for building permanent residential structures.

Individual

Single owner applying for personal residential development.

Partnership

Two or more co-owners applying jointly. Requires all individual documents plus partnership agreement deed.

Educational Non-Agricultural Land

Designated for building schools, colleges, universities, coaching centres, and other educational institutions.

Granted

Land directly granted by the government for educational purposes.

Semi-Granted & Private

Partially government-aided or entirely privately owned land converted for educational use.

Industrial Non-Agricultural Land

Used for factories, manufacturing plants, warehouses, processing units, and industrial estates. Conversion requires strict compliance with environmental norms.

Commercial Non-Agricultural Land

Used for shops, offices, malls, hotels, restaurants, and commercial complexes. This is the most common type of conversion in urban areas.
